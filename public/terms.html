Terms of Use

InfoRetriever gives registered users access to POEMs (Patient Oriented Evidence that Matters), summaries of clinical research written for primary care clinicians, together with tools to search them and to prepare Evidence Summaries. By creating an account or using InfoRetriever, you agree to these terms.

Your account

Each account is for one person. Keep your password private and do not share your account. You must give a working email address that belongs to you.

Permitted use

You may read POEMs, search them, and print or download them in the amounts the site allows, for your own clinical, teaching or study purposes. You may quote brief passages with attribution to InfoRetriever.

Prohibited use

  • Copying, downloading or collecting POEMs in bulk, whether by hand or by automated means (scripts, crawlers, scrapers, browser automation or similar tools).
  • Using any content from InfoRetriever to train, fine-tune, evaluate or supply data to an artificial intelligence or machine learning system, or adding it to a dataset for such use.
  • Republishing, selling, sublicensing or redistributing POEMs, or making them available to people who do not have their own account.
  • Trying to get around the site's usage limits or security, including by opening several accounts.

Usage limits

To protect the collection, the number of POEMs you can open, searches you can run, and printouts and Evidence Summaries you can download each hour and each day is limited. Accounts that repeatedly reach the daily limits, or that appear to be used for prohibited purposes, may be locked without notice.

Medical information

POEMs summarize published research to support, not replace, clinical judgment. They are not medical advice for any individual patient. Always consider the original research and the circumstances of each patient.
