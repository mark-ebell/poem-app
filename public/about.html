InfoRetriever

About InfoRetriever

InfoRetriever brings together the evidence a primary care clinician needs at the point of care: short summaries of new research (POEMs), a comprehensive textbook of clinical topics, calculators for interpreting the history, physical examination and diagnostic tests, and clinical decision rules. One search on the Search tab looks through all of them.

POEMs

POEMs (Patient-Oriented Evidence that Matters) are concise, independent, evidence-based summaries of research that matters to primary care physicians and their patients. Each POEM answers a clinical question, describes the study and its quality, and ends with a bottom-line answer. The collection holds more than 8,000 POEMs published since 1994, and new POEMs are added every month. How POEMs are selected, written and reviewed.

Topic chapters

The Browse Chapters tab is a textbook of over 800 clinical topics organized the way clinicians think about a problem: background, screening and prevention, diagnosis, treatment, prognosis and special populations. Each chapter links to the POEMs, calculators and decision rules that apply to it. Each chapter is updated every year.

Differential diagnosis

For more than 100 common symptoms and presentations, the Differential Diagnosis tab lists the likely causes, ranked by how often they are found in primary care.

History and physical calculators

The History & Physical tab shows how much a symptom or sign raises or lowers the probability of a disease, using likelihood ratios from published studies. It covers about 1,500 findings for about 190 combinations of symptom and disease. Choose a starting (pretest) probability and see the probability after a positive or negative finding.

Diagnostic test calculators

The Diagnostic Tests tab does the same for laboratory tests, imaging and other diagnostic tests: about 2,200 tests for nearly 600 combinations of symptom and disease, each with its sensitivity, specificity, likelihood ratios and source.

Risk scores

The Risk Scores tab has more than 400 clinical decision rules and risk scores, from cardiovascular risk to pneumonia severity, in more than 40 categories. Each one calculates its result as you answer its questions, and describes the study it comes from, the population studied and how it was validated.

Using the information

InfoRetriever supports clinical judgment; it does not replace it. Its content is not medical advice for any individual patient. Always consider the original research and the circumstances of each patient. See the Terms of Use.
